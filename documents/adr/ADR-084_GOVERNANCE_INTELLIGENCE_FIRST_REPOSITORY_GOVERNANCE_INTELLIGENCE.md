# ADR-084: Governance-Intelligence-First, Decision-Aware, and Standards-Based Repository Governance Intelligence Architecture

## Status

LOCKED — PERMANENT

## Date

Sprint 2C, Step 94

## Context

EduBridge OS requires a centralized, governance-intelligence-first approach to repository development governance intelligence that prevents unauthorized implementations, enforces governance intelligence standards, ensures transparency, and maintains human oversight in all governance intelligence decisions. The Repository Development Governance Intelligence Module is not merely a collection of governance intelligence rules or decision intelligence checks — it is a comprehensive intelligence layer that ensures every future domain, service, intelligence module, API, integration, and platform capability must inherit validated governance intelligence standards before development activities are authorized.

## Decision

The Repository Development Governance Intelligence Module is established as the permanent owner of repository-wide governance intelligence standards, repository decision intelligence standards, governance intelligence analysis, development governance intelligence readiness requirements, and repository governance intelligence registries throughout EduBridge OS. Every future implementation, domain, service, intelligence module, API, integration, and platform capability must inherit validated governance intelligence standards before development activities are authorized.

## Constraints

### Prohibited Actions
- Governance intelligence bypass mechanisms
- Automatic governance intelligence approvals
- Unauthorized implementations
- Hardcoded governance decisions
- Cross-domain ownership violations
- Repository-wide service duplication
- Unauthorized repository modifications
- Non-transparent governance intelligence decisions

### Repository Development Governance Intelligence Never
- Authorizes unauthorized implementations
- Bypasses governance intelligence requirements
- Overrides governance policies
- Replaces mandatory governance intelligence mechanisms
- Duplicates repository-wide services

## Consequences

- Repository Development Governance Intelligence Module becomes permanent owner of repository-wide governance intelligence standards
- No future implementation may bypass governance intelligence requirements
- All implementations must inherit validated governance intelligence standards
- Complete audit trail for every governance intelligence operation
- Human oversight required at every decision point
- Governance-intelligence-first architecture with decision-aware governance
- Repository development governance intelligence remains independently testable, observable, explainable, maintainable, scalable, recoverable, interoperable, privacy-preserving, governance-aware, and policy-compliant
