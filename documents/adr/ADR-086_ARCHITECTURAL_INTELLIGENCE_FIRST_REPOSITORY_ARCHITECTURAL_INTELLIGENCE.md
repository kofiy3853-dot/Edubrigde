# ADR-086: Architectural-Intelligence-First, Architecture-Aware, and Standards-Based Repository Architectural Intelligence Architecture

## Status

LOCKED — PERMANENT

## Date

Sprint 2C, Step 96

## Context

EduBridge OS requires a centralized, architectural-intelligence-first approach to repository development architectural intelligence that prevents unauthorized implementations, enforces architectural intelligence standards, ensures transparency, and maintains human oversight in all architectural intelligence decisions. The Repository Development Architectural Intelligence Module is not merely a collection of architectural intelligence rules or architectural decision checks — it is a comprehensive intelligence layer that ensures every future domain, service, intelligence module, API, integration, and platform capability must inherit validated architectural intelligence standards before development activities are authorized.

## Decision

The Repository Development Architectural Intelligence Module is established as the permanent owner of repository-wide architectural intelligence standards, repository architectural decision standards, architectural intelligence analysis, development architectural intelligence readiness requirements, and repository architectural intelligence registries throughout EduBridge OS. Every future implementation, domain, service, intelligence module, API, integration, and platform capability must inherit validated architectural intelligence standards before development activities are authorized.

## Constraints

### Prohibited Actions
- Architectural intelligence bypass mechanisms
- Automatic architectural intelligence approvals
- Unauthorized implementations
- Hardcoded architectural decisions
- Cross-domain ownership violations
- Repository-wide service duplication
- Unauthorized repository modifications
- Non-transparent architectural intelligence decisions

### Repository Development Architectural Intelligence Never
- Authorizes unauthorized implementations
- Bypasses architectural intelligence requirements
- Overrides governance policies
- Replaces mandatory architectural intelligence mechanisms
- Duplicates repository-wide services

## Consequences

- Repository Development Architectural Intelligence Module becomes permanent owner of repository-wide architectural intelligence standards
- No future implementation may bypass architectural intelligence requirements
- All implementations must inherit validated architectural intelligence standards
- Complete audit trail for every architectural intelligence operation
- Human oversight required at every decision point
- Architectural-intelligence-first architecture with architecture-aware governance
- Repository development architectural intelligence remains independently testable, observable, explainable, maintainable, scalable, recoverable, interoperable, privacy-preserving, architecture-aware, and policy-compliant
