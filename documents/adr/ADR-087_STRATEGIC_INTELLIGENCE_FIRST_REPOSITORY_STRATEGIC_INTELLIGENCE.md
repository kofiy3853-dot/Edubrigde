# ADR-087: Strategic-Intelligence-First, Strategy-Aware, and Standards-Based Repository Strategic Intelligence Architecture

## Status

LOCKED — PERMANENT

## Date

Sprint 2C, Step 97

## Context

EduBridge OS requires a centralized, strategic-intelligence-first approach to repository development strategic intelligence that prevents unauthorized implementations, enforces strategic intelligence standards, ensures transparency, and maintains human oversight in all strategic intelligence decisions. The Repository Development Strategic Intelligence Module is not merely a collection of strategic intelligence rules or strategic decision checks — it is a comprehensive intelligence layer that ensures every future domain, service, intelligence module, API, integration, and platform capability must inherit validated strategic intelligence standards before development activities are authorized.

## Decision

The Repository Development Strategic Intelligence Module is established as the permanent owner of repository-wide strategic intelligence standards, repository strategic decision standards, strategic intelligence analysis, development strategic intelligence readiness requirements, and repository strategic intelligence registries throughout EduBridge OS. Every future implementation, domain, service, intelligence module, API, integration, and platform capability must inherit validated strategic intelligence standards before development activities are authorized.

## Constraints

### Prohibited Actions
- Strategic intelligence bypass mechanisms
- Automatic strategic intelligence approvals
- Unauthorized implementations
- Hardcoded strategic decisions
- Cross-domain ownership violations
- Repository-wide service duplication
- Unauthorized repository modifications
- Non-transparent strategic intelligence decisions

### Repository Development Strategic Intelligence Never
- Authorizes unauthorized implementations
- Bypasses strategic intelligence requirements
- Overrides governance policies
- Replaces mandatory strategic intelligence mechanisms
- Duplicates repository-wide services

## Consequences

- Repository Development Strategic Intelligence Module becomes permanent owner of repository-wide strategic intelligence standards
- No future implementation may bypass strategic intelligence requirements
- All implementations must inherit validated strategic intelligence standards
- Complete audit trail for every strategic intelligence operation
- Human oversight required at every decision point
- Strategic-intelligence-first architecture with strategy-aware governance
- Repository development strategic intelligence remains independently testable, observable, explainable, maintainable, scalable, recoverable, interoperable, privacy-preserving, strategy-aware, and policy-compliant
