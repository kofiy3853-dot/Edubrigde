# Student Success Intelligence Standards

LOCKED — PERMANENT

> **Scope**: The Student Success Intelligence Management Module is the permanent owner of repository-wide student success intelligence standards throughout EduBridge OS. All future academic success planning, educational progression capabilities, student achievement services, learning milestone planning, and student development capabilities must comply with repository-wide governance, validation, explainability, auditing, monitoring, and lifecycle management policies defined by this module.

## Student Success Intelligence Standards

### Permanent Ownership

The Student Success Intelligence Management Module permanently owns:

- Academic Success Planning Intelligence
- Student Achievement Intelligence
- Educational Progression Intelligence
- Learning Success Intelligence
- Student Development Intelligence
- Cross-Domain Student Success Integration Standards

### Scope

Every future capability involving academic progression planning, student achievement intelligence, educational milestone management, learning success planning, engagement intelligence, and student development capabilities must consume repository-wide Student Success Intelligence standards.

### Responsibilities

- Provide academic success planning standards for all domains
- Ensure student achievement intelligence is repository-wide
- Maintain educational progression intelligence policies
- Support cross-domain student success integration
- Enforce student success validation and governance

### Constraints

- No future domain may duplicate Student Success Intelligence ownership
- No student success validation bypass mechanisms
- No autonomous academic decisions
- No guaranteed academic outcomes
- No guaranteed graduation outcomes
- Human oversight required at every decision point
