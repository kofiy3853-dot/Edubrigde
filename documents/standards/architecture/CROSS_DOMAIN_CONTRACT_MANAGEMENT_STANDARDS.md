# Cross-Domain Contract Management Standards

LOCKED — PERMANENT

> **Scope**: The Cross-Domain Contract Management Module is the permanent owner of all repository-wide contracts, service agreements, ownership boundaries, integration expectations, and implementation constraints throughout EduBridge OS. Every future domain, service, intelligence module, and platform capability must communicate through validated contracts. No domain may directly depend on another domain's internal implementation details.

### Permanent Ownership

The Cross-Domain Contract Management Module permanently owns:

- Contract Intelligence Engine

### Scope

No future domain is permitted to own cross-domain contract management. Every future domain, service, intelligence module, and platform capability must communicate through validated contracts.

### Responsibilities

- Provide cross-domain contract standards for all domains
- Ensure service agreements are repository-wide
- Maintain ownership boundary standards

- Enforce contract-first architecture

### Constraints

- Human oversight required at every decision point
