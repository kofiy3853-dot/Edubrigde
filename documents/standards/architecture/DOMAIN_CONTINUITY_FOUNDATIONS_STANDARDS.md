# Domain Continuity Foundations Standards

LOCKED — Permanent — ADR-114

## Purpose

Establish domain continuity foundation standards, continuity compatibility standards, continuity readiness standards, continuity validation standards, and domain continuity registry standards for all future domain continuity implementations in EduBridge OS.

## Domain Continuity Foundations Architecture

```
Domain Continuity Foundations
        |
Continuity Standards Engine
        |
Continuity Compatibility Standards Engine
        |
Continuity Validation Standards Engine
        |
Continuity Readiness Standards Engine
        |
Domain Continuity Registry
        |
Future Domain Continuity Implementations
```

**Note:** Cross-cutting concerns are shared across all modules via repository-wide services. This module owns ONLY its unique responsibilities.

## Cross-Domain Integration

```
Identity      Learning      Financial      Analytics      Communication
    |              |              |               |                |
    uses           uses           uses            uses             uses
    v              v              v               v                v
Domain Continuity Foundations Module (permanent owner)
```

## 1. Domain Continuity Foundation Standards

### Permanent Ownership
Domain Continuity Foundations Module owns all domain continuity foundation standards.

### Standards Categories
- Domain Continuity Standards
- Continuity Modeling Standards
- Continuity Isolation Standards
- Continuity Specification Standards
- Continuity Lifecycle Standards
- Future Continuity Standards

### Every Continuity Profile MUST Permanently Support
- Domain Continuity Information
- Continuity Modeling Information
- Continuity Isolation Information
- Continuity Specification Information
- Continuity Lifecycle Information

### Continuity Profiles Remain Independently Configurable

### Every Future Continuity MUST Inherit Repository and Continuity Foundation Standards

## 2. Continuity Compatibility Standards

### Permanent Ownership
Domain Continuity Foundations Module owns all continuity compatibility standards.

### Required Continuity Rules
- All continuity MUST follow compatibility standards
- All cross-domain continuity MUST be registered
- All continuity specifications MUST be documented
- All continuity compatibility MUST be validated
- All continuity changes MUST be auditable

### Continuity Compatibility Requirements
- Continuity specifications MUST follow naming conventions
- Continuity definitions MUST be versioned
- Continuity dependencies MUST be declared
- Continuity boundaries MUST be explicit
- Continuity interfaces MUST be documented

## 4. Continuity Readiness Standards

### Permanent Ownership
Domain Continuity Foundations Module owns all continuity readiness standards.

### Required Readiness Rules
- All continuity MUST meet readiness criteria before implementation

### Continuity Readiness Requirements
