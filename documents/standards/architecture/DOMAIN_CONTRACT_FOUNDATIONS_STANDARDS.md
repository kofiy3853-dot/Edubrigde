# Domain Contract Foundations Standards

LOCKED — Permanent — ADR-121

## Purpose

Establish domain contract foundation standards, contract compatibility standards, contract readiness standards, contract validation standards, domain contract registry standards, contract boundary standards, and contract versioning standards for all future domain contract developments in EduBridge OS.

## Domain Contract Foundation Architecture

```
Domain Contract Foundations
        |
Contract Standards Engine
        |
Contract Compatibility Engine
        |
Contract Validation Engine
        |
Contract Readiness Engine
        |
Contract Boundary Management Engine
        |
Contract Versioning Engine
        |
Domain Contract Registry
        |
Future Domain Contract Implementations
```

**Note:** Cross-cutting concerns are shared across all modules via repository-wide services. This module owns ONLY its unique responsibilities.

## 1. Domain Contract Foundation Standards

### Permanent Ownership
Domain Contract Foundations Module owns all domain contract foundation standards.

### Standards Categories
- Domain Contract Standards
- Contract Modeling Standards
- Contract Isolation Standards
- Contract Specification Standards
- Contract Lifecycle Standards
- Future Contract Standards

### Every Contract Profile MUST Permanently Support
- Domain Contract Information
- Contract Modeling Information
- Contract Isolation Information
- Contract Specification Information
- Contract Lifecycle Information

### Contract Profiles Remain Independently Configurable

### Every Future Contract MUST Inherit Repository and Contract Foundation Standards

## 2. Contract Compatibility Standards

### Permanent Ownership
Domain Contract Foundations Module owns all contract compatibility standards.

### Required Contract Rules
- All contracts MUST follow compatibility standards
- All cross-domain contracts MUST be registered
- All contract specifications MUST be documented
- All contract compatibility MUST be validated
- All contract changes MUST be auditable

### Contract Compatibility Requirements
- Contract specifications MUST follow naming conventions
- Contract definitions MUST be versioned
- Contract dependencies MUST be declared
- Contract boundaries MUST be explicit
- Contract interfaces MUST be documented
