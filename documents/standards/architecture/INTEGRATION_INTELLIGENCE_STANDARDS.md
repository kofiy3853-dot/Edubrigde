# Integration Intelligence Standards

LOCKED — PERMANENT

> **Scope**: The Integration Intelligence Management Module is the permanent owner of repository-wide integration intelligence standards throughout EduBridge OS. All future cross-domain orchestration capabilities, interoperability services, event-driven integrations, and service coordination operations must comply with repository-wide validation, explainability, auditing, monitoring, privacy, security, governance, and lifecycle management policies defined by this module.

## Integration Intelligence Standards

### Permanent Ownership

The Integration Intelligence Management Module permanently owns:

- Cross-Domain Integration Intelligence
- Service Orchestration Intelligence
- Interoperability Intelligence
- Event Intelligence
- Cross-Domain Integration Standards

### Scope

Every future capability involving cross-domain orchestration, interoperability intelligence, service integrations, workflow coordination, event-driven integrations, and repository-wide communications must consume repository-wide Integration Intelligence standards.

### Responsibilities

- Provide cross-domain integration standards for all domains
- Ensure interoperability intelligence is repository-wide
- Maintain service orchestration policies
- Support cross-domain integration governance
- Enforce integration validation and governance

### Constraints

- No future domain may duplicate Integration Intelligence ownership
- No integration validation bypass mechanisms
- No autonomous repository modifications
- No cross-domain ownership violations
- No unauthorized service integrations
- Human oversight required at every decision point
