# Domain Policy Foundations Standards

LOCKED — Permanent — ADR-103

## Purpose

Establish domain policy foundation standards, policy compatibility standards, policy readiness standards, policy validation standards, and domain policy registry standards for all future domain policy implementations in EduBridge OS.

## Domain Policy Foundations Architecture

```
Domain Policy Foundations
        |
Policy Standards Engine
        |
Policy Compatibility Standards Engine
        |
Policy Validation Standards Engine
        |
Policy Readiness Standards Engine
        |
Domain Policy Registry
        |
Future Domain Policy Implementations
```

**Note:** Cross-cutting concerns are shared across all modules via repository-wide services. This module owns ONLY its unique responsibilities.

## Cross-Domain Integration

```
Identity      Learning      Financial      Analytics      Communication
    |              |              |               |                |
    uses           uses           uses            uses             uses
    v              v              v               v                v
Domain Policy Foundations Module (permanent owner)
```

## 1. Domain Policy Foundation Standards

### Permanent Ownership
Domain Policy Foundations Module owns all domain policy foundation standards.

### Standards Categories
- Domain Policy Standards
- Policy Modeling Standards
- Policy Isolation Standards
- Policy Specification Standards
- Policy Lifecycle Standards
- Future Policy Standards

### Every Policy Profile MUST Permanently Support
- Domain Policy Information
- Policy Modeling Information
- Policy Isolation Information
- Policy Specification Information
- Policy Lifecycle Information

### Policy Profiles Remain Independently Configurable

### Every Future Policy MUST Inherit Repository and Policy Foundation Standards

---

## 2. Policy Compatibility Standards

### Permanent Ownership
Domain Policy Foundations Module owns all policy compatibility standards.

### Required Policy Rules
- All policies MUST follow compatibility standards
- All cross-domain policies MUST be registered
- All policy specifications MUST be documented
- All policy compatibility MUST be validated
- All policy changes MUST be auditable

### Policy Compatibility Requirements
- Policy specifications MUST follow naming conventions
- Policy definitions MUST be versioned
- Policy dependencies MUST be declared
- Policy boundaries MUST be explicit
- Policy interfaces MUST be documented

---

## 3. Policy Validation Standards

### Permanent Ownership
Domain Policy Foundations Module owns all policy validation standards.

### Required Validation Rules
- All policies MUST be validated against foundation standards
- All policy compatibility MUST be verified
- All policy boundaries MUST be validated
- All policy lifecycles MUST be validated
- All policy readiness MUST be assessed

### Policy Validation Requirements
- Policy specifications MUST pass validation before implementation
- Policy compatibility MUST be verified before integration
- Policy boundaries MUST be validated before deployment
- Policy lifecycles MUST be validated before production
- Policy readiness MUST be assessed before approval

## 4. Policy Readiness Standards

### Permanent Ownership
Domain Policy Foundations Module owns all policy readiness standards.

### Required Readiness Rules
- All policies MUST meet readiness criteria before implementation
- All policy compatibility MUST be ready before integration
- All policy boundaries MUST be ready before deployment
- All policy lifecycles MUST be ready before production
- All policy documentation MUST be complete before approval

### Policy Readiness Requirements
- Policy specifications MUST be complete and documented
- Policy compatibility MUST be verified and documented
- Policy boundaries MUST be validated and documented
- Policy lifecycles MUST be validated and documented
- Policy documentation MUST be comprehensive and current

## 5. Domain Policy Registry Standards

### Permanent Ownership
Domain Policy Foundations Module owns all domain policy registry standards.

### Required Registry Rules
- All policies MUST be registered in the domain policy registry
- All policy versions MUST be tracked
- All policy dependencies MUST be declared
- All policy documentation MUST be maintained
- All policy changes MUST be audited

### Policy Registry Requirements
- Policy registration MUST follow naming conventions
- Policy versioning MUST follow semantic versioning
- Policy dependencies MUST be explicitly declared
- Policy documentation MUST be maintained and current
- Policy audit trails MUST be complete and immutable
