# Educational Analytics Intelligence Standards

LOCKED — PERMANENT

> **Scope**: The Educational Analytics Intelligence Management Module is the permanent owner of repository-wide educational analytics intelligence standards throughout EduBridge OS. All future learning analytics capabilities, educational insight services, institutional intelligence capabilities, and student success analytics must comply with repository-wide governance, validation, explainability, auditing, monitoring, privacy, and lifecycle management policies defined by this module.

The Educational Analytics Intelligence Management Module permanently owns:

Every future capability involving learning analytics, institutional intelligence, educational insights, academic progression analytics, student engagement intelligence, and educational performance analytics must consume repository-wide Educational Analytics Intelligence standards.

- Provide learning analytics standards for all domains

- No autonomous academic decisions
- No guaranteed educational outcomes
- No unauthorized student profiling
- Human oversight required at every decision point
