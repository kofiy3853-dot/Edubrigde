# Financial Intelligence Standards

LOCKED — PERMANENT

> **Scope**: The Financial Intelligence Management Module is the permanent owner of repository-wide educational financial intelligence standards throughout EduBridge OS. All future educational financial planning, tuition intelligence capabilities, student financial wellness services, international education cost planning, and educational expense management capabilities must comply with repository-wide governance, validation, explainability, auditing, monitoring, and lifecycle management policies defined by this module.

## Financial Intelligence Standards

### Permanent Ownership

The Financial Intelligence Management Module permanently owns:

- Educational Budget Planning Intelligence
- Tuition Planning Intelligence
- Student Financial Wellness Intelligence
- Educational Cost Intelligence
- International Financial Planning Intelligence
- Educational Expense Forecasting Intelligence
- Financial Readiness Intelligence
- Cross-Domain Financial Integration Standards

### Scope

Every future capability involving educational budgeting, tuition planning, student financial wellness, cost-of-education intelligence, international financial planning, educational expense forecasting, and financial readiness services must consume repository-wide Financial Intelligence standards.

### Responsibilities

- Provide educational financial planning standards for all domains
- Ensure student financial wellness intelligence is repository-wide
- Maintain educational cost intelligence policies
- Support cross-domain financial integration
- Enforce financial validation and governance

### Constraints

- No future domain may duplicate Financial Intelligence ownership
- No financial validation bypass mechanisms
- No autonomous financial decisions
- No guaranteed funding outcomes
- No guaranteed financial approvals
- Human oversight required at every decision point
