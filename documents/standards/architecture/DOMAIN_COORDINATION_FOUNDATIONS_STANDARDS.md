# Domain Coordination Foundations Standards

LOCKED — Permanent — ADR-096

## Purpose

Establish domain coordination foundation standards, coordination compatibility standards, coordination readiness standards, coordination validation standards, and domain coordination registry standards for all future domain coordination in EduBridge OS. This module creates the coordination foundations that govern how future domains, platforms, and services coordinate architectural responsibilities through explicit coordination contracts, validation standards, and repository-wide coordination policies.
